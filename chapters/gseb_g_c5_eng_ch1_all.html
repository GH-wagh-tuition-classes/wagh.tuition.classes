Unit 1: Ice-cream Man

Read the poem by Rachel Field. Tap the stanzas below to see translations in Gujarati and Hindi, and to hear the pronunciation!

Line 1: When summer's in the city,
જ્યારે શહેરમાં ઉનાળો હોય છે,
जब शहर में गर्मी होती है,
Line 2: And brick's a blaze of heat,
અને ઈંટો ગરમીથી ભડકે બળે છે,
और ईंटें गर्मी से धधकती हैं,
Line 3: The Ice-cream Man with his little cart
આઈસ્ક્રીમ વાળો તેની નાની ગાડી સાથે
आइसक्रीम वाला अपनी छोटी गाड़ी के साथ
Line 4: Goes trundling down the street.
શેરીમાં ગબડતો ગબડતો જાય છે.
सड़क पर लुढ़कता हुआ जाता है।
Line 5: Beneath his round umbrella,
તેની ગોળ છત્રી નીચે,
उसके गोल छाते के नीचे,
Line 6: Oh, what a joyful sight,
અરે, કેવું આનંદદાયક દ્રશ્ય છે,
ओह, क्या आनंददायक दृश्य है,
Line 7: To see him fill the cones with mounds
તેને કોનમાં ઢગલાબંધ આઈસ્ક્રીમ ભરતો જોવો
उसे कोन में आइसक्रीम के ढेर भरते देखना
Line 8: Of cooling brown or white:
ઠંડા કથ્થઈ અથવા સફેદ રંગના:
ठंडे भूरे या सफेद रंग के:
Line 9: Vanilla, chocolate, strawberry,
વેનીલા, ચોકલેટ, સ્ટ્રોબેરી,
वैनिला, चॉकलेट, स्ट्रॉबेरी,
Line 10: Or chilly things to drink
અથવા પીવા માટેની ઠંડી વસ્તુઓ
या पीने के लिए ठंडी चीजें
Line 11: From bottles full of frosty-fizz,
ઠંડા અને ફીણવાળા પીણાંથી ભરેલી બોટલોમાંથી,
ठंडे और झागदार ड्रिंक से भरी बोतलों से,
Line 12: Green, orange, white, or pink.
લીલો, નારંગી, સફેદ અથવા ગુલાબી.
हरा, नारंगी, सफेद या गुलाबी।
Line 13: His cart might be a flower bed,
તેની ગાડી કદાચ ફૂલોની ક્યારી હોઈ શકે,
उसकी गाड़ी शायद फूलों की क्यारी हो सकती है,
Line 14: Of roses and sweet peas,
ગુલાબ અને મીઠા વટાણાની,
गुलाब और मीठे मटर की,
Line 15: The way the children cluster round
જે રીતે બાળકો તેની આસપાસ એકઠા થાય છે
जिस तरह से बच्चे उसके चारों ओर इकट्ठा होते हैं
Line 16: As thick as honeybees.
મધમાખીઓની જેમ ગાઢ રીતે.
मधुमक्खियों की तरह घने रूप में।

Q1. In which season is ice cream popular?
Answer: Ice cream is popular in summer season.
Q2. Who feels joyful on seeing the Ice-cream Man?
Answer: The children feel joyful on seeing the Ice-cream Man.
Q3. Name the different flavours of ice cream the Ice-cream Man has in his cart.
Answer: The Ice-cream Man has vanilla, chocolate, and strawberry flavours in his cart.
Q4. What are the two things that the Ice-cream Man is selling?
Answer: The Ice-cream Man is selling ice cream and chilled drinks (frosty-fizz).
Q5. What is the ice-cream cart compared to in the poem?
Answer: The ice-cream cart is compared to a flower bed of roses and sweet peas.
Q1. Having an ice cream on a hot summer day is exciting and enjoyable. Write about some of the exciting and enjoyable things that one can do in the winter season.
⛄ ❄️ 🧤
Answer: We can play in the snow, go ice skating, build a snowman, sit near a bonfire, and drink hot chocolate or hot soup.
Q2. List as many summer activities as you can.
🏊‍♂️ 🏖️ 🥤
Answer: Swimming, going to the beach, drinking cold lassi and juices, playing indoor games like Ludo, and eating lots of ice cream!
