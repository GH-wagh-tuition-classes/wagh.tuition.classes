A. Answer the following questions:

Q1. What is Papa searching for?
Answer: Papa is searching for his spectacles.
Q2. What are the possible places where Papa can find it?
Answer: He can possibly find them inside his pockets, under his chair, or near the window.
Q3. At last, where does Papa find his lost item?
Answer: At last, Papa finds his lost spectacles right there on his head!

B. Think and discuss:

Q1. Apart from reading, guess some other chores that Papa cannot do without the spectacles.
Answer: Driving the car, working on the computer, cooking, or fixing small things around the house.
Q2. What makes your papa happy?
Answer: Spending time with family, when I help with chores, and when I study properly makes papa happy. (Share your own answer too!)
